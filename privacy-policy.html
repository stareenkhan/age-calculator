Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data ChronoAge does — and does not — collect.

Calculations stay in your browser

Our chronological age calculator and all related tools run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The dates you enter, including any date of birth, are processed locally on your device. We never transmit, log, or store the dates you enter. Closing or refreshing the page clears them.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require accounts or sign-in.
  • We do not collect names, emails, or birth dates through the calculator.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Analytics & cookies

If we use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic, it is configured to avoid collecting personally identifiable information. Any cookies used are limited to essential site functionality. You can block cookies in your browser without affecting the calculator.

Third-party links

Our guides may link to trusted external resources (for example, the CDC or NIH). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites.
