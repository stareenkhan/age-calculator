How Old Am I?

Wondering how old am I right now? Enter your date of birth to see your exact age in years, months, and days, how many days you have lived, and a countdown to your next birthday.

—

Your birth date never leaves your browser.

Key takeaways

  • "How old am I" is answered by your chronological age — calendar time since birth.
  • Your exact age depends on the day and month, not just the birth year.
  • This tool also shows total days lived and your next-birthday countdown.

How the "how old am I" calculation works

Your age is the difference between today's date and your date of birth. The calculator subtracts the two dates precisely — years, then months, then days — so the answer is accurate even across leap years. If you want to know your age on a specific past or future day, change the "as of this date" field.

"If I was born in… how old am I?"

A common question is something like "how old is someone born in 2007?" The answer changes depending on whether the birthday has already happened this year, which is why entering the full date of birth gives the only exact result. The same logic answers "what year was I born?" in reverse — subtract your age from the current year, adjusting for whether your birthday has passed.

Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2007?

Enter your 2007 date of birth above and the calculator shows your exact current age. It depends on the exact day and month, not just the year.

How do I find out how many days old I am?

The result includes your total days lived alongside years, months, and days. It counts every calendar day, including leap days.

Can I check my age on a future date?

Yes — set the "as of this date" field to any future date to see how old you will be then.
