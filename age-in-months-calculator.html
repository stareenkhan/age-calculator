How to calculate age in months

To find age in months, multiply the number of whole years by 12 and add any extra months. A child who is 5 years and 7 months old is therefore 5 × 12 + 7 = 67 months. The remaining days (the part of the current month already elapsed) are usually reported separately. This age in months calculator performs the conversion automatically and keeps the remaining days for you.

When age in months matters

Pediatric growth charts, developmental screeners, and cognitive tests are normed by month because young children change quickly. Reporting "67 months" instead of "5 years" places a child in the correct, narrower scoring band. Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate age in months?

Multiply whole years by 12 and add extra months. For example, 5 years and 7 months is 5 × 12 + 7 = 67 months. The calculator above also shows the remaining days.

Why do tests use age in months?

Standardized assessments group norms by month, so age in months gives a more precise scoring band than whole years, especially for children.

What does 5;7 mean?

It is the years;months notation: 5 years and 7 months. It equals 67 total months.
