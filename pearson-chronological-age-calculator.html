What the Pearson chronological age calculator does

Pearson publishes a chronological age calculator to support its assessment products. You enter the examinee's date of birth and the test date, and it returns the chronological age in years and months — the value used to look up the correct norms when converting raw scores. The math is the standard date subtraction explained in our how to calculate chronological age guide.
