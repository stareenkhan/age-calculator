## 2. NLP / Entity Analysis (GEO + Semantic Layer)

For Generative Engine Optimization (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) and classic NLP relevance, the page must establish these **entities and their relationships** explicitly.

### Core entities (must appear & be defined on-site)
- **Chronological age** — time elapsed from birth date to a reference/test date (years, months, days).
- **Biological age** — physiological age vs. calendar age (contrast entity).
- **Corrected / adjusted age** — chronological age minus prematurity weeks (pediatrics).
- **Test date / assessment date** — the reference date used in standardized testing.
- **Date of birth (DOB)** — input variable.
- **Age in months** — required output for SLP/psych assessment.

### Salient related entities (from competitor `Entities` column + clusters)
`Pearson (Organization)`, `WISC`, `WAIS`, `Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale`, `Brigance`, `ASQ`, `Gesell`, `ESI`, `PAR`, `Cornell University`, `Super Duper`, `Home Speech Home`, `LAUSD`, `occupational therapy`, `speech-language pathology (SLP)`, `developmental assessment`.

### Semantic / LSI terms to weave into copy
date of birth, years months days, exact age, age difference, leap year, reference date, test administration date, raw age, age equivalent, standardized testing, normed assessment, premature/preterm, gestational age, corrected age, decimal age, age in weeks, "as of" date.

### NLP triples to state plainly (helps AI Overview extraction)
- *Chronological age is the amount of time that has passed since a person's date of birth.*
- *Chronological age is calculated by subtracting the date of birth from the test date.*
- *Speech-language pathologists use chronological age in years and months to score standardized assessments.*
- *Corrected age equals chronological age minus the number of weeks a baby was born premature.*
- *Biological age differs from chronological age because it reflects health and physiology, not the calendar.*
