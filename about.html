About ChronoAge

ChronoAge builds free, accurate, privacy-first date and age tools — starting with our flagship chronological age calculator. This page explains who we are, how we calculate, and how we keep content trustworthy.

Why we built this

Calculating chronological age by hand is fiddly and error-prone, and many existing tools are slow, ad-heavy, or ask you to upload personal data. We wanted a fast, clean calculator that works on any device, returns the exact figure clinicians and educators need (years, months, and total months), and keeps every date entirely on your device.

Who creates the content

Our guides are written and reviewed by the ChronoAge Editorial Team — contributors with backgrounds in education, speech-language assessment, and software. We draw on first-hand experience scoring standardized tests, where age is recorded as years;months to the day of administration, and we cross-check medical topics (such as corrected age for preterm infants) against public guidance from bodies like the CDC, AAP, and NIH.

Our calculation method

Every result uses an exact calendar algorithm: we subtract the date of birth from the reference date column by column, borrowing the real number of days in each month and accounting for leap years. We deliberately avoid the "divide total days by 365" shortcut because it drifts off by days. You can read the full method in how to calculate chronological age.

Accuracy & updates

We review our tools and articles regularly and display a "Last updated" date on every page. If you spot an error, please contact us — we correct issues promptly.

Privacy first

All calculations run in your browser with JavaScript. We never transmit or store your date of birth. See our privacy policy for details.
